Draw the back face of the box whose front is this picture, bound in
the violet leather of the shield on the front: the same fine pebbled
grain, the same deep violet, lit softly at the centre and darkening
evenly toward the edges, with the same burnished gold tooling.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 5:7 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image. Elixir violet (#4B275F) is the dominant colour
of the illustration.

This face carries no subject: no shield, no bird, no figure, no
picture of anything. The face is the violet leather, even across the
whole face, and a single fine gold-tooled fillet runs round it: its
top side at three hundredths of the height, its bottom side at ninety
hundredths of the height, its sides three hundredths of the width in
from each edge, meeting in plain square corners. Nothing on the face
is lettered: no title, no caption, no signature, no placeholder text,
no glyphs, no words of any kind anywhere on the face.

Across the middle of the face the leather continues, bare, and one
seal is blind-embossed into it as a watermark, centred on the width,
its centre at fifty-eight hundredths of the height, two fifths of the
width wide: a circle made of two laurel branches meeting at the top
and at the bottom, and inside the circle one firebird in outline, the
bird of the front with the same silhouette, and nothing else — pressed
into the leather with no gold, a step darker than the leather around
it, barely visible, the one mark on this face. It carries no
lettering.

Across the bottom edge, over the full width, from ninety-two
hundredths of the height to the bottom edge, a band of the same
leather a step darker than the face, with nothing printed on it; the
fillet's bottom side sits above it.

Every mark on the face is printed material or tooling, and the marks
named above are all of them.
Accent: the front's — the bronze gold of the title; nothing else on
this face has a colour of its own.
