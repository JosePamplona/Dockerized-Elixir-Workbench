Late-1980s console game box cover art. Painted airbrush illustration with
the slightly stiff, heroic look of North American 8-bit era packaging:
hard airbrush gradients, chunky specular highlights, visible matte
cardboard grain.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 4:5 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image. The ground of the illustration, edge to edge,
is one tone that reads clearly lighter or darker in value than Elixir
violet (#4B275F).

Hero illustration, painted airbrush: an explorer on a rock ledge at
dawn, holding a great map open toward the viewer. The ground of the
painting is the dawn sky: a pale hard airbrush gradient from light gold
at the horizon to pale sky blue at the top edge, far lighter than the
land. From the top edge of the image down to a fifth of the height, the
sky runs across the whole width as an even field of that pale blue, low
in detail, with no cloud and no sun in it.

Behind the explorer, a wide valley painted in hard gradients falls away
to a line of blue mountains along the horizon at 0.40 of the height
down, running across the whole width, the rising sun hidden behind them
and lighting their ridges gold.

The explorer stands on the ledge facing the viewer, full length, at
0.50 of the width: the top of their hat at 0.24 of the height down,
their boots at 0.78 of the height down, their figure 0.54 of the height
tall. A young explorer in a wide-brimmed hat and a long travelling
coat, lit hard from the left, with chunky white specular highlights on
the hat brim, the shoulder and the boots; their face is visible above
the map, looking down at it.

The map is held open in both hands before the explorer at chest height,
its painted face turned toward the viewer: a single sheet of cream
vellum 0.60 of the width across, centred at 0.50 of the width, its top
edge at 0.42 of the height down and its bottom edge at 0.64, curling a
little at its two rolled ends where the hands hold it. Most of its face
is painted in full colour — a coast along its left side, blue rivers,
green forest, brown mountains, a few small towns — and a dotted red
trail wanders across it from its lower left corner, through the forest,
the mountains and the towns. In three small places inside the painted
land the sheet is bare cream vellum, each outlined in thin brown ink,
each about a tenth of the map's width across, and the trail passes
around them: one at the upper left of the map, one just below its
centre, one at its right. At the centre of each bare patch, one small
question mark in brown ink.

The ledge is dark grey-brown rock, lit gold along its top edge and
falling into deep shadow toward the bottom edge. The bottom fifth of the
image is that rock in deep shadow, low in detail, and the lockup sits
on it.

Title lockup across the lower part of the image, over that shadow:
"COVERAGE" on one line, set large in heavy chrome-edged capitals with
a hard drop shadow, catching the gold dawn light, centred on the width
and three quarters of the width wide; and beneath it, in smaller
capitals of the same lettering, the subtitle "WHERE NOBODY LOOKED".

In the top right corner of the image, a small rectangular flash in the
same heavy capitals reading "80% MIN" at foreground size, its centre at
0.84 of the width and 0.08 of the height down.

The title, the subtitle, the flash and the three question marks on the
map are all the lettering in the image.

The top left of the artwork is composed as a quiet area: low detail, no
focal element, an even field of tone — the pale blue of the upper sky.

Every mark on the face is printed illustration or type, and the marks
named above are all of them.
