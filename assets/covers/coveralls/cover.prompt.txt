Early-1990s CD-ROM game cover art. Chrome and lens flare over an
airbrushed starfield: extruded metallic lettering with deep bevels,
rainbow specular edges, a perspective grid, saturated gradients pushed
just past taste, a glossy laminated finish.

Flat front face reproduced straight on, filling the frame edge to edge:
not a photograph of a physical box, no perspective, no drop shadow, no
surrounding background, no visible spine or side panels. Proportions 5:7
portrait.

Top band across the full width, dark with a thin chrome rule, reading
"DOCKERIZED ELIXIR WORKBENCH" in condensed sans-serif caps.

Hero illustration: a vast plain of rectangular glass code panels laid
out in rows, receding in perspective toward an airbrushed starfield. A
compact polished chrome lens hangs above the plain, two thirds of the way
across the frame from the left, and one vertical curtain of acid lime
green light falls from the exact centre of the lens straight down onto
the plain, so that lens and curtain share the same vertical axis. Every
panel to the left of the light curtain glows acid lime green from
within, except a few panels that stay dark glass. Every panel to the
right of the light curtain is dark glass. Near the right edge of the
plain stands one thin vertical red laser line, from the top of the frame
to the floor, and a large holographic plate hovers beside its foot, just
above the horizon, at foreground size, reading "COVERAGE TARGET 80%". A
lens flare bursts off the chrome rim of the lens. The lens is compact, no
wider than a third of the frame, and the starfield above and to the
right of it stays open.

Title lockup in the lower third: "COVERALLS" set large in extruded chrome
lettering with deep bevels and rainbow specular edges, and beneath it a
smaller subtitle "EVERY GAP SHOWS".

Bottom left: a small rectangular badge reading "1 QUALITY GATE".

Top left: a small silver holographic security sticker.
A faint shrink-wrap sheen across the whole face.

The top right of the artwork is composed as a quiet
area: open starfield, low detail, no focal element, an even field of
tone.

Palette: Elixir violet (#4B275F) as the dominant colour of the
composition, CRT phosphor haze in the background. No photographic
elements. No real brand marks or logos.
Accent: acid lime green.
