# dashboard — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's NEED.md first, then its README and
DESIGN.md, and nothing else. The era block of the winner goes into
`cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what follows is the hero paragraph each
era would put in it.

## The need

*Something is slow in dev and you're guessing.* Before: logs,
`:observer` if you remember how, a hunch. After: `/dev/dashboard` —
processes, memory, ETS, every request timed, the repo's queries — live
in the browser, only in dev.

## The register

**Candid** — the mechanism shows what is happening while it happens,
and leaves the judgement to you. Subtitles disclose. Added for this
cartridge: deadpan documents; candid shows you the needle moving.

Title `DASHBOARD`. Subtitle `STOP GUESSING`. Badge `/DEV/DASHBOARD` —
the route. Accent **gauge orange** or the era's nearest, unused on the
shelf. No furniture unless the era's own column has one.

The counts that are true: **seven** pages; **one** route; **one**
needle in the red at a time; dev **only**. The strings a hero may
carry at foreground size, one per hero: `/dev/dashboard`, `STOP
GUESSING`, `metrics`, `live_dashboard`. Where a hand is on a
forehead, it is the guess; where a needle is, it is the answer.

## The recommendation

**1, the early carton.** The need is *one needle in the red, and now
you can see which* — and the carton's board with seven dials and one
callout line is that fact with nothing added; the candid register is
a ruled line from the needle to the route. Every other era needs
either a figure to stop guessing or chrome to make a gauge
interesting.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. The carton has no cover; gettext's proposals recommend it too,
and two is not the signal. If one of them should move, 3 — the single
gauge — keeps this one's stance in one object.
