Mid-1990s PC game cover art. Dense oil-painted illustration in the
fully-rendered style of the 256-colour era: deep chiaroscuro, heavy
detail carried down into the shadows, dramatic single-source lighting,
extruded fantasy lettering with a hard bevel, a glossy laminated finish.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 5:7 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image.

Back face layout. This face carries no subject: no figure, no scene, no
picture of anything. The face is the printed board in deep Elixir
violet (#4B275F), even across the whole face, under the glossy
laminate. One thing on it is lettered, and only one: the words
"DOCKERIZED ELIXIR WORKBENCH", set in extruded fantasy lettering with a
hard bevel, gold, on two lines — "DOCKERIZED" on the first line and
"ELIXIR WORKBENCH" on the second — at foreground size, centred on the
width, inside a rectangle that runs from ten hundredths of the width to
ninety hundredths of the width and from four hundredths of the height
to twenty hundredths of the height. Nothing else on the face is
lettered: no title, no caption, no signature, no placeholder text, no
glyphs, no words of any kind anywhere else, neither inside the frames
nor outside them.

Two empty inset frames, each outlined by a thin gilt rule, each filled
with one flat near-black tone, unlit, with nothing in it, each frame
three wide by two tall. Both run from twenty-four hundredths of the
height down to forty-four hundredths of the height. The left one runs
from fourteen hundredths of the width to forty-four hundredths of the
width; the right one from fifty-six hundredths of the width to
eighty-six hundredths of the width. Between the two frames, and
between each frame and the side edge, the board is bare.

Below them the printed board continues across the middle third of the
face, and one round guild seal is printed into it in the same violet
ink as the board, centred on the width, its centre at sixty-two
hundredths of the height, a quarter of the width across, barely darker
than the board it sits on — a watermark rather than a print, and the
one mark on this face. Inside the seal's rim there is one open book
seen from above, its two pages plain, and nothing else. It carries no
lettering.

Across the bottom edge, over the full width, a band of the same board
printed in a darker ink, about a fifteenth of the height of the face.

Palette: Elixir violet (#4B275F) as the dominant colour of the
composition. CRT phosphor haze in the background. Every mark on the
face is printed material or type, and the marks named above are all
of them.
Accent: vermilion ink (#D9432B), the light on the gold of the
lettering and of the frames' rules.
