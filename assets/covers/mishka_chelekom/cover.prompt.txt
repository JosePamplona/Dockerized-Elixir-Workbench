THIS HERO IS MANUAL. There is no prompt that regenerates it.

It was generated once from a prompt — proposal 1 of `eras.md`, *the
straight way*, kept as `_archived/art/cover-1.prompt.txt` beside the
take it made — and what came back was right in every body and count
and wrong in one way: the prompt's decimal positions were drawn on the
face as dimension figures with measuring lines, and the paragraph that
asks for a quiet corner was lettered into that corner. The picture is
continued from there, in conversation with the generator, and the
image itself is the source.

A manual hero can only be **continued**, never regenerated. Every turn
is the current `art/hero.jpg` plus the last entry below, and each entry
is written here *before* the take that tests it. The hero is archived
before anything overwrites it, without exception.

Everything downstream is unchanged: `covers.py pad`, the expansion,
`covers.py cut`, `covers.py stamp`, the invariants, the counts.
