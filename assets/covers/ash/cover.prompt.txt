Mid-1990s PC game cover art. Dense oil-painted illustration in the
fully-rendered style of the 256-colour era: deep chiaroscuro, heavy
detail carried down into the shadows, dramatic single-source lighting,
extruded fantasy lettering with a hard bevel, a glossy laminated finish.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 4:5 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image. The ground of the illustration, edge to edge,
is one tone that reads clearly lighter or darker in value than Elixir
violet (#4B275F).

Hero illustration: a great workshop at night in deep chiaroscuro,
oil-painted, detail carried down into the shadow — benches, tools hung
on the walls, and a forge glowing at the right edge of the image at
half the height down, the one light source in the picture. In the
centre of the image, seen from behind and a little to the left, with
the top of the head at a third of the height down and the figure
standing as tall as half the height of the image, one patron in a long
dark coat holding out a specification: a sheet of parchment unrolled
between both hands, its face turned toward the viewer, lit by the
forge, and on it one line of engraved-looking capitals at foreground
size reading "mix igniter.install", and nothing else on the parchment.
Facing the patron across a long workbench, six craftsmen in leather
aprons standing in a row, the nearest at half the width of the image
and the farthest at the right edge, fading into the dark, each holding
one tool raised in one hand — a hammer, a square, a caliper, a chisel,
a file, a brush — their faces lit from the parchment and the forge.
Behind the row of craftsmen, half built and half in shadow, a machine
of brass and iron rising from the bench to the top edge of the image,
its brass catching the forge light on its nearest edges. The forge
light dies into the dark before it reaches the left edge and the top
edge of the image; the ground where the light does not reach is
near-black with a violet cast. The floor of the workshop takes the
bottom fifth of the image, dark boards with the lockup on them.

Title lockup across the lower part of the image: "ASH" set large in
extruded fantasy lettering with a hard bevel, lit from the forge, and
beneath it a smaller subtitle "SAY WHAT. NOT HOW.".

In the upper left corner of the image, a small rectangular flash in the
era's lettering reading "EXISTING APP" at foreground size.

The line on the parchment, the title, the subtitle and the flash are
all the lettering in the image.

The upper right corner of the artwork is composed as a quiet area: low
detail, no focal element, an even field of tone — the dark of the
rafters, out of the forge's reach.

Every mark on the face is printed illustration or type, and the marks
named above are all of them.
