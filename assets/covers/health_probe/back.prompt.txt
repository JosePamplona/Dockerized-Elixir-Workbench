Draw the back face of the box whose front is this picture, in exactly
this printing: the same dark plum leather, lit at the centre and
darkening evenly toward the edges, the same fine grain, the same thin
embossed frame with rounded corners a little inside the edges, the
same gold classical serif lettering.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 5:7 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image. Elixir violet (#4B275F) is the dominant colour
of the illustration.

This face carries no subject: no bell, no lozenges, no figure, no
picture of anything. The face is the dark plum leather of the front,
even across the whole face, with the embossed frame running round it
as on the front: its top side at four hundredths of the height, its
bottom side at ninety hundredths of the height, its sides two
hundredths of the width in from each edge. One thing on it is
lettered, and only one: the words "DOCKERIZED ELIXIR WORKBENCH", in
exactly the gold classical serif capitals of the title on the front,
printed the same way, on two lines — "DOCKERIZED" on the first line
and "ELIXIR WORKBENCH" on the second — at foreground size, centred on
the width, inside a rectangle that runs from ten hundredths of the
width to ninety hundredths of the width and from eight hundredths of
the height to twenty-two hundredths of the height. Nothing else on the
face is lettered: no title, no caption, no signature, no placeholder
text, no glyphs, no words of any kind anywhere else, anywhere on the
face.

Across the middle of the face the leather continues, bare, and one
publisher's colophon is blind-embossed into it as a watermark, centred
on the width, its centre at fifty hundredths of the height, a fifth of
the width wide: an upright oval outline, and inside it one small desk
bell drawn in outline and nothing else — pressed into the leather, a
step darker than the leather around it, barely visible, the one mark
on this face. It carries no lettering.

Across the bottom edge, over the full width, from ninety-three
hundredths of the height to the bottom edge, a band of the same
leather a step darker than the face, with nothing printed on it; the
frame's bottom side sits above it.

Every mark on the face is printed material or type, and the marks
named above are all of them.
Accent: the front's — the gold of the lettering; nothing else on this
face has a colour of its own.
