MANUAL HERO. The image in art/hero.jpg is the source, the way
overlay.xcf is: it was made in conversation with the generator,
turn after turn, starting from proposal 3 (editorial cover) in
eras.md and the prompt archived beside cover-2 — the brass desk bell
on an even ground, the two routes engraved on its base, the title
lockup in a classical serif, the platform line, the badge flash. It
cannot be regenerated, only continued: every change asked of the
image from here on is logged below, before the take that tests it,
and the previous image is archived before the new one overwrites it.

What the image carries beyond that prompt, read off it on 2026-08-28
(the turns that produced it were not logged — the log starts here):

- The ground is a dark plum leather, near black at the edges and
  lit at the centre — violet in hue, darker than the board in value.
- Three platform lozenges across the top, each with the platform's
  own mark drawn by the generator beside the name; the same three
  marks again as inlays on the bell's base.
- A cutaway window in the dome showing the mechanism; four thin
  callout lines to small technical captions; a faint grid behind.
- A magazine credit line at the bottom right, small.
- The badge "EVERY ENV" as a brushed-metal plate under the subtitle.
