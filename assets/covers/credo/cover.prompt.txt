# credo — manual hero

The hero is **manual**: made by the author outside the repository, in
conversation with the generator, from proposal 7 in `eras.md` (the
classroom, the author's own) and the classroom drawing that was its
reference. The image is the source, the way `overlay.xcf` is: it cannot
be regenerated, only continued, so it is archived before anything
overwrites it — `_archived/art/hero-manual-1.png` is the state of
2026-09-21 18:24, as it arrived (`art/hero.png`, 1126×1397, 4:5);
`art/hero.jpg` is that image converted, untouched.

The prompted box before it — take 5 of the console hero, its padded
canvas, expansion, cut and sealed cover — is archived as `cover-4*`,
this file's previous text as `cover-4.prompt.txt`. `art/lockup.png`, cut
from take 4, stays: the title is still composited, not painted.

The turns that made the hero were not logged; the log starts here, and
every entry is written before the take that tests it. Each turn is the
current `art/hero.jpg` plus the last entry below.

Everything downstream is unchanged: the proportion `covers.py window`
names (4:5), `pad`, `cut`, `stamp`, the invariants, the counts.

## What the hero carries (read off the image, 2026-09-21)

An empty classroom in one-point perspective, lit by afternoon sun from
a window on the left: a green blackboard on the far wall, a wooden
teacher's desk with an empty blue chair behind it, a tiled floor of
cream and blue running to the foot. On the board, a chalk table of
Credo's five groups, each with a coloured dot: `[C] Consistency`,
`[D] Design`, `[F] Refactoring`, `[R] Readability`, `[W] Warning`.
Ceiling with two lit fixtures, a wall clock above the board, a plant
and books on a side table at the left, a door and a `CLASSROOM RULES`
poster at the right, a `LEARN` pennant by the window. No title, no
badge, no furniture, no wear, no version and no date.

Lettering beyond the board: the poster's four rules, `LEARN`, and two
book spines whose text is not words. Open, for the author's eye.

The ground is warm cream and ochre, clearly lighter than the violet.
