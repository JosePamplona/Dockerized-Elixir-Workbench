# ecto — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's README and DESIGN.md and nothing else. The
era block of the winner goes into `cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what
follows is the hero paragraph each era would put in it.

## The register

**Ominous** — the mechanism is superior to the viewer, and withholds.
A repo, an adapter, a database that from the moment of the insert
remembers everything the project tells it; inserted once, because
changing the database of a project with data is a migration, not a
flag. What the project writes, it cannot un-write. Subtitles
threaten.

Title `ECTO`. Subtitle `NOTHING IS FORGOTTEN`. Badge `4 ADAPTERS` —
Postgres, MySQL, MSSQL, SQLite3, the closed list the design states.
Accent **cold jade** or the era's nearest: the green of a database
that answers, unused on the shelf. No furniture unless the era's own
column has one.

The counts that are true: **four** adapters; **four** files created;
**three** environments configured; **one** variable the release reads
(`DATABASE_URL`, or a path); **one** repo in the supervision tree;
inserted **once**. The strings a hero may carry at foreground size,
one per hero: `Repo`, `DATABASE_URL`, `ecto.migrate`, `SELECT`,
`priv/repo`. Where four things appear they are the adapters; where one
vault, it is the repo.

## The recommendation

**6, the big box.** This is the only base cartridge with weight: four
files, fourteen changed, a variable the release will refuse to boot
without, a database that will hold the project's data and cannot be
swapped by flag. The oil painting is the one era that carries detail
down into shadow, and the ajar door with the light behind it is the
insert exactly — opened once, and what goes through does not come
back.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. **This would be the third big box**, which the record names as
the signal for a rule. If that rule is wanted now, 2 — the monolith
on the grid — is the runner-up, and the home-computer era has only a
manual descendant.
