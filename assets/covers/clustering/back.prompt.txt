Draw the back face of the box whose front is this picture, in exactly
this drawing style — the same line, the same soft shading, the same
palette, the same riveted metal plates and the same wood.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 5:7 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image.

This face carries no subject: no terminals, no platform scene, no
figure, no picture of anything. The face is a flat printed board in
deep Elixir violet (#4B275F), even across the whole face, drawn with
the same soft shading as the picture. One thing on it is lettered, and
only one: the words "DOCKERIZED ELIXIR WORKBENCH", engraved on one
riveted metal plate exactly like the title plate at the bottom of the
front — the same brushed metal, the same rivets at its corners, the
same gold lettering — on two lines, "DOCKERIZED" on the first line and
"ELIXIR WORKBENCH" on the second, at foreground size, the plate
centred on the width, inside a rectangle that runs from ten hundredths
of the width to ninety hundredths of the width and from four
hundredths of the height to twenty hundredths of the height. Nothing
else on the face is lettered: no title, no caption, no signature, no
placeholder text, no glyphs, no words of any kind anywhere else,
anywhere on the face.

Across the middle of the face the violet board continues, bare, and
one glass vacuum tube like the two in the front picture is printed
into it as a watermark, unlit, in a violet one step darker than the
board, centred on the width, its centre at fifty hundredths of the
height, a fifth of the width wide — barely darker than the board it
sits on, the one mark on this face. It carries no lettering.

Across the bottom edge, over the full width, a band of the same
planked wood as the front's platform, seen straight on, about a
fifteenth of the height of the face, with nothing printed on it.

Palette: Elixir violet (#4B275F) as the dominant colour of the
composition. Every mark on the face is printed material or type, and
the marks named above are all of them.
Accent: the front's — the warm amber of the tubes on the plate's
gold, the cyan of the beams nowhere on this face.
