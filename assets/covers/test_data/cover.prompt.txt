Mid-1980s literary game cover art. Restrained editorial design rather
than illustration: an even ground of a single colour, one carefully lit
object centred with generous space around it, serif type set small, a
subtle deboss, clean printing on coated stock.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 4:5 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image. The ground of the illustration, edge to edge,
is one tone that reads clearly lighter or darker in value than Elixir
violet (#4B275F).

Hero illustration: one object lit carefully on an even ground, a paper
doll chain. The ground is one even light warm grey, edge to edge, lit
softly from the upper left, its only texture the fine tooth of the
coated stock. Across the middle of the image, from 0.10 to 0.90 of the
width, stands a chain of seven paper figures cut from one folded strip
of cream paper, hand in hand, each figure 0.30 of the height tall, their
feet on one line at 0.66 of the height down, their centres evenly
spaced across that span. The folds between them show as faint creases
where the hands join. Six of the figures are plain cream paper, and
each carries one small detail drawn lightly in graphite pencil — a hat
on the first, a collar on the second, round glasses on the third, a bow
on the fifth, a scarf on the sixth, a cap on the seventh — so no two
are alike and every one is complete, with a face of two dots and a
line. The fourth figure, at the centre, 0.50 of the width, is coloured
in with coloured pencil: a violet dress and a warm face, the only
colour in the chain, and across its chest, hand-lettered in dark
graphite capitals at foreground size, "ANA". Under the chain lies one
soft close shadow falling down and to the right, as of paper standing a
finger's width off the ground. Above the chain, from the top edge down
to 0.30 of the height, the image is that same even warm grey ground,
calm and unbroken; below the shadow, the same ground continues to the
title.

Title lockup across the lower part of the image, centred, its baseline

serif, dark violet, 0.56 of the width across, and beneath it a smaller

In the top left corner of the image, its centre at 0.14 of the width

"ANA", the title, the subtitle and the flash are all the lettering in

The top right corner of the artwork is composed as a quiet area: low
detail, no focal element, an even field of the warm grey ground.
