# live — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's NEED.md first, then its README and
DESIGN.md, and nothing else. The era block of the winner goes into
`cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what follows is the hero paragraph each
era would put in it.

## The need

*Your page should react without reloading.* Before: html with its
`LiveSocket` lines commented out; every click a full request. After:
the socket connected on load, `phx-click`, a LiveView mountable from
the router. Not for a project without esbuild: no client in the
browser.

## The register

**Attentive** — the mechanism watches the page and moves it as you
act, and never makes you wait for the whole thing again. Subtitles
promise. Added for this cartridge.

Title `LIVE`. Subtitle `NO RELOAD` — the two words the need turns on.
Badge `/LIVE` — the socket. Accent **pulse cyan** or the era's
nearest, unused on the shelf. No furniture unless the era's own
column has one.

The counts that are true: **one** socket; **zero** files created;
**every** click answered in place; **one** spinner gone. The strings
a hero may carry at foreground size, one per hero: `phx-click`,
`NO RELOAD`, `/live`, `LiveSocket`. Where a page reloads it is the
before; where a hand touches it and only the touched thing moves, it
is the after.

## The recommendation

**2, the home computer.** The need is *only the thing you touched
moves*, and a flat slab on a grid with one button displaced and the
rest unchanged shows it without a figure or a mechanism — the motion
arcs are the whole register. The carton's crossed-out reload says the
before; the home-computer picture is the after, which is what a box
front is for.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. The home-computer era has no printed cover; tailwind's proposals
recommend it too, and name the CD-ROM entry as their alternative.
