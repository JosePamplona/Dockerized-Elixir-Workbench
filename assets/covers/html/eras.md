# html — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's README and DESIGN.md and nothing else. The
era block of the winner goes into `cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what
follows is the hero paragraph each era would put in it.

## The register

**Hospitable** — the mechanism receives whoever arrives and has
already laid everything out for them. A browser pipeline of six plugs,
a route at `/`, a page controller with its view and template, a root
layout, the core components, a logo on the wall: a JSON API that
grows a front door and a room behind it. Subtitles invite.

Title `HTML`. Subtitle `COME IN`. Badge `GET /` — the one route the
design states. Accent **doorway amber** or the era's nearest: light
through an open door, unused on the shelf. No furniture unless the
era's own column has one.

The counts that are true: **ten** files created; **six** plugs in the
browser pipeline; **one** route, `/`; **one** layout with a root and an
app; **one** logo; **two** tests. The strings a hero may carry at
foreground size, one per hero: `GET /`, `:browser`, `<.flash>`,
`home.html.heex`, `Layouts.app`. Where six things appear in a row they
are the pipeline; where one door, it is the route.

## The recommendation

**4, the console entry.** html is the one base cartridge with a
*figure* in it — a page that greets a visitor — and the console era is
the one that paints a figure with a face. The innkeeper in the doorway
is the browser pipeline receiving a request, the laid table its six
plugs, and the register needs no explaining. The other five have to
say "welcome" with a door and no host.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. Console has one cover; this would be its second. If two consoles
beside two big boxes reads as drift, 2 — the doorframe on the grid —
says the same thing with no host, and the home-computer era has only
a manual descendant.
