## What the layout has to answer

Three things differ from the pattern `covers.py back` assumes, and each
is a layout decision rather than a fault — a plate is judged
**composed**, not bare (`back.md`; the inert editorial plate that
composed well is the precedent):

* **The band is at 0.94, thin and dark.** `NAME_Y` about 1.32 in width
  fractions, and `INSTALL_ROW=beside` as on version_manager and ash,
  whose bands were also short.
* **The copy's field is the machinery panel.** The pattern assumes the
  middle third is a watermark the blurb can sit over; here it is a lit
  mechanism at stddev 17–24. Compose it and look: if the blurb does not
  read, the answer is the copy's field, not the plate.
* **The frames go in the ceiling**, the flattest field: two at 0.40 of
  the width from about 0.06, ending above two fifths of the face, which
  is what keeps a back writable.
