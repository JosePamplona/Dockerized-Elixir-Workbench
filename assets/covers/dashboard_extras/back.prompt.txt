Draw the back face of the box whose front is this picture, in exactly
this printing: the same plain deep ground that lies under the title at
the foot of the front — one dark even tone with the same fine
photographic grain, printed the same matte way — a little lighter
toward the middle of the face and darkening evenly toward the edges.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 5:7 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image. Elixir violet (#4B275F) is the dominant colour
of the illustration.

This face carries no subject: no instrument, no dial, no machine hall,
no cabinets, no lamps, no figure, no picture of anything. The face is
the front's plain dark ground, even across the whole face, printed in
deep Elixir violet where the front's is blue-black: the violet of
#4B275F at the middle of the face, deepening to a violet near black at
the four edges, the same grain throughout. Nothing on the face is
lettered: no title, no caption, no signature, no placeholder text, no
glyphs, no words of any kind anywhere on the face.

Across the middle of the face the ground continues, bare, and one
publisher's colophon is printed into it as a watermark, centred on the
width, its centre at fifty hundredths of the height, a fifth of the
width wide: an upright oval outline, and inside it one small round
dial drawn in outline — a circle, inside its upper half a short arc of
seven tick marks, and one straight needle from the circle's centre
pointing to the upper right — and nothing else. It is a step darker
than the ground around it, barely visible, a watermark rather than a
print, and the one mark on this face. It carries no lettering.

Across the bottom edge, over the full width, from ninety-three
hundredths of the height to the bottom edge, a band of the same ground
a step darker than the face, a violet near black, with nothing printed
on it.

Every mark on the face is printed material or type, and the marks named
above are all of them.
Accent: the front's — the warm amber of the lit dials; nothing on this
face carries it, and nothing else on this face has a colour of its own.
