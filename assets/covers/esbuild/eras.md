# esbuild — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's README and DESIGN.md and nothing else. The
era block of the winner goes into `cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what
follows is the hero paragraph each era would put in it.

## The register

**Heroic** — the mechanism triumphs, and the viewer is invited to join
it. Three sources, a vendor script and every import in `deps/`, and
out of it one file the page loads; a watcher that rebuilds it the
moment a source changes; the placeholder comment that said *copy the
scripts yourself* taken away. Subtitles command.

Title `ESBUILD`. Subtitle `ONE FILE TO SHIP`. Badge `JS/APP.JS` — the
entry, the one path the design states. Accent **arc yellow** or the
era's nearest: the colour of a spark, unused on the shelf. No furniture
unless the era's own column has one.

The counts that are true: **three** sources created; **three**
aliases (`assets.setup`, `assets.build`, `assets.deploy`); **one**
watcher; **one** bundle out; **one** placeholder replaced. The strings
a hero may carry at foreground size, one per hero: `app.js`, `bundle`,
`--watch`, `import`. Where three things appear they are the sources;
where one, it is the bundle.

## The recommendation

**2, the home computer.** Esbuild is three things becoming one on a
flat floor, and the era that composes with hard-edged solids sliding
on a grid draws exactly that — the cubes are the sources, the big cube
is the bundle, and nothing has to be dramatised into a forge or a
foundry. The heroic register is in the motion lines, not in a figure.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. The home-computer era has a manual descendant and no printed
cover; this would be its first from a prompt.
