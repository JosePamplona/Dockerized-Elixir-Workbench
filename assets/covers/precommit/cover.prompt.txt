THIS HERO IS MANUAL. There is no prompt that regenerates it.

It was made in conversation with the generator, outside the repository,
and the image itself is the source — as `overlay.xcf` is for the
overlay. It started from proposal 3, the editorial cover (`eras.md`),
and left the era behind: what came back is a photographic door, not a
printed editorial ground. The register did not move — *scrupulous*, the
thing held back at your own door — and neither did the subject the six
proposals share: one thing finished and about to go, and one small
fitting, reachable by the person it stops.

A manual hero can only be **continued**, never regenerated. Every turn
is the current `art/hero.jpg` plus the last entry below, and each entry
is written here *before* the take that tests it. The hero is archived
before anything overwrites it, without exception.

Everything downstream is unchanged: `covers.py pad`, the expansion,
`covers.py cut`, `covers.py stamp`, the invariants, the counts.

### 1 — 2026-09-20, the image as it arrived (art/hero.jpg, 825x1024)

A dark wooden door, ajar, seen straight on and filling the frame: the
jamb and the dark gap at the left, the door's stiles and one raised
panel at the right. Across the gap, a brass security chain at the end
of its travel — the knob hard against the closed end of its slotted
plate — the plate screwed to the door at the middle right, the chain
running up and to the left to its mounting plate on the jamb. Warm
light from the right rakes across the grain; the room beyond the gap is
unlit. The lettering is in the art: "PreCommit" in a white serif at
0.72 of the width and 0.57 of the height down, and "Not yet." beneath
it, smaller, with its full stop.

What it keeps, and what it changed, against the take it replaces
(`_archived/art/cover-2.jpg`, the editorial hero):

* The chain, at the end of its travel, is still the whole argument —
  and here it has the door the editorial take could not have, since
  that era's method is one object on an empty ground.
* No unit badge. The typeset `--badge "4 CHECKS"` was stamped once and
  taken off: a white chip on photographic art reads as a sticker from
  another system, and this hero composes its own lettering.
* The title is set as `PreCommit`, not the uppercase the title slot
  asks of a prompt. The image is the source; it is not corrected.
* The margins are not flat, so the expansion repeats the hero's own
  edge rows and columns (`expand.py`) rather than asking a second turn
  for wood grain. Everything it smears falls under the board: 124px
  above, 66px below, against the band's 124 and the strip's 65.
