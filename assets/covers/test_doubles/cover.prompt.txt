# test_doubles — manual hero

The hero is **manual**: made in conversation with the generator,
outside the repository, starting from proposal 5 (CD-ROM, early 1990s)
in `eras.md`, whose prompt did not convince. The image is the source,
the way `overlay.xcf` is: it cannot be regenerated, only continued, so
`art/hero.jpg` is archived before anything overwrites it —
`_archived/art/hero-1.jpg` is the state of 2026-09-20 09:51.

The turns that made it were not logged; the log starts here, and every
entry is written before the take that tests it. Each turn is the
current `art/hero.jpg` plus the last entry below.

Everything downstream is unchanged: the proportion `covers.py window`
names (4:5), `pad`, `cut`, `stamp`, the invariants, the counts.

## What the hero carries (read off the image, 2026-09-20)

Three bodies on a ship's bridge, left to right: a man standing in a
lit glass capsule (`THE REAL ONE`), a white android of the same build
(`COPIED`), and a cyan wireframe figure of the same build standing on
a projector pad (`BUILT TO SPEC`). The real one is flesh and the two
doubles are made, which is the metaphor of `eras.md` in the bodies
rather than in the captions. Lockup `TEST DOUBLES` / `NEVER THE REAL
ONE` across the foot; badge `2 DOUBLES` on a screen panel at the top
left; a banner across the top reading `Mimic · Mox · Hammox`. No
furniture, no wear, no version and no date.

## The log

### 1 — the top right has to be the seal's (2026-09-20)

Measured on the hero, in fractions of its own width and height: the
banner's lettering runs from 0.073 to **0.857** of the width at 0.03 to
0.14 of the height, and the caption row runs to 0.928 of the width at
0.16 to 0.25. The seal straddles a top corner and the composition owes
it a quarter-disc of calm; there is lettering in all four corners of
this hero, so at 0.23 it eats `Hammox` and the top of `BUILT TO SPEC`
at top right, and the lockup at either bottom corner. At 0.15 it clears
the captions and eats only the last two letters of `Hammox`.

The banner is also the one thing on the face that contradicts another:
the badge says `2 DOUBLES` and the banner names three. The box's option
is `--double mimic|mox`, two values; **Hammox is not a third double**,
it is what `--type-check` puts in Mox's place, and it belongs on the
back where that switch is explained.

So one change answers both:

> In the banner across the top, the text reads **`Mimic · Mox`** — the
> same lettering, the same banner, two names instead of three, ending
> by 0.60 of the width. The top right of the image, from 0.72 of the
> width to the right edge and from the top edge down to 0.15 of the
> height, is the bridge's dark wall: an even field, no lettering and no
> focal element in it.

Everything else in the image stays exactly as it is.

### 2 — the expansion goes to the generator (2026-09-20)

Supersedes the hand expansion. `expand.py` stays beside this file as
what was done first and as the fallback: it continues the hero's own
edge rows and columns, which holds above and below — those bands are
near-flat dark — but at the sides it repeats a column of the bridge's
wall panelling, and this overlay has **no board at the sides** (`covers.py
window`: board top 105, bottom 55, left 0, right 0), so those 19px
show on the finished cover. A painted continuation reads better there
than a repeated column.

`./assets/covers/covers.py pad test_doubles` has written the canvas:

    assets/covers/test_doubles/art/padded.jpg   863x1214

The hero sits in it at +19+124, untouched, with flat grey around it:
124px above, 66px below, and 19px at each side inside the window. Send
that canvas to the generator **in the same conversation the hero was
made in**, with the turn below, and save what comes back as

    assets/covers/test_doubles/art/expanded.jpg

--- the second turn ---

The flat grey border around this picture is blank. Paint it by
continuing the picture outward into it on every side, to the edges —
the bridge's dark ceiling and its upper bulkhead continue above, the
deck's floor plating and the lit console strip continue below, and at
each side the wall's panelling and its recessed screens run on to the
edge. Keep the picture inside exactly as it is, and add nothing new: no
figure, no object, no lettering.

---

Then `covers.py cut test_doubles` and `covers.py stamp test_doubles
--corner tr --inside --size 0.15`. If `cut` reports the expansion short
on a side, the generator cropped or moved the picture and the turn is
rerun; if it keeps its own output size, that is expected — it is cut to
whatever scale it comes back at.
