# dashboard — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's README and DESIGN.md and nothing else. The
era block of the winner goes into `cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what
follows is the hero paragraph each era would put in it.

## The register

**Deadpan** — the mechanism is documented apparatus and needs no
selling. Seven pages of instruments — home, OS, metrics, request
logger, processes, ETS, and Ecto stats when there is a repo — on one
dev route, riding the `/live` socket, with a request logger plug in
the endpoint; three files changed, nothing created. Subtitles state.

Title `DASHBOARD`. Subtitle `EVERY NEEDLE LIVE`. Badge `/DEV/DASHBOARD`
— the route, the one path the design states. Accent **gauge orange**
or the era's nearest: a needle on a dial, unused on the shelf. No
furniture unless the era's own column has one.

The counts that are true: **three** files changed and **zero**
created; **one** route; **one** plug; **one** socket; **seven** pages.
The strings a hero may carry at foreground size, one per hero:
`/dev/dashboard`, `metrics`, `live_dashboard`, `RequestLogger`. Where
seven things appear they are the pages; where one dial, it is the
route.

## The recommendation

**1, the early carton.** The dashboard is the one cartridge that *is*
an instrument panel, and the carton era is the one that draws
instruments as a schematic — seven dials, seven labels, a cable — with
nothing to dramatise. Deadpan on a line drawing is the register with
no acting at all, which is what seven pages of telemetry are.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. The carton has no cover — but gettext's proposals recommend it
too. If the card index takes it, 3 — the one gauge — is the same
instrument with the same stance, and editorial has only a manual
descendant.
