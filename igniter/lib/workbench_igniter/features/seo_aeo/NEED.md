# seo_aeo

Your pages should be found, and quoted correctly, by a search engine and by an assistant answering someone's question.

**Before:** every page goes out with the same title and no description; nothing tells a crawler which pages exist or which address is the real one, and what an assistant says about you is whatever it could guess.

**After:** each page says what it is — title, description, canonical address, structured data — the application publishes its own sitemap and crawling rules, and a review in the repository says what was checked, for search engines (SEO) and for answer engines (AEO).

**Not for:** content, keywords or a promise of ranking — it covers what the application emits, not what the pages say.
