# live — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's README and DESIGN.md and nothing else. The
era block of the winner goes into `cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what
follows is the hero paragraph each era would put in it.

## The register

**Attentive** — the mechanism watches the page and moves it as you
act. One socket at `/live` that stays open, a `LiveSocket` in the
browser that connects on load, and every click answered without a
reload; it builds on html and adds no file, only lines that were
written commented out and come alive. Subtitles promise. Added for
this cartridge: none of the landmarks has the mechanism *responding*
to the viewer.

Title `LIVE`. Subtitle `IT MOVES WHEN YOU DO`. Badge `/LIVE` — the
socket, the one path the design states. Accent **pulse cyan** or the
era's nearest: a connection light, unused on the shelf. No furniture
unless the era's own column has one.

The counts that are true: **zero** files created and **six** changed;
**one** socket; **two** JS commands (show, hide); **one** config
block that is the mark; every `LiveSocket` line uncommented. The
strings a hero may carry at foreground size, one per hero:
`phx-click`, `LiveSocket`, `/live`, `connect()`, `phx-r`. Where a wire
appears it is the socket; where a thing moves, it is the page.

## The recommendation

**2, the home computer.** live is a hand touching a page and the page
answering, and the era that draws flat solids on a grid with motion
arcs is the one that shows *movement* without a figure to carry it —
the cord to the socket, the touch, the three arcs. The other eras
either need a puppeteer or a clockwork to say the same thing.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. The home-computer era has a manual descendant and no printed
cover — but esbuild's proposals recommend it too, and html's names it
as runner-up; if it is taken by then, 1 — the cord and the window —
is the carton's version of the same wire.
