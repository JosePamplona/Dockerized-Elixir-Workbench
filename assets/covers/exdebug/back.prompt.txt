Late-1980s console game box cover art. Painted airbrush illustration with
the slightly stiff, heroic look of North American 8-bit era packaging:
hard airbrush gradients, chunky specular highlights, visible matte
cardboard grain.

Flat cover illustration filling the entire image edge to edge, all of
it in one plane: the four edges of the image are the four edges of the
illustration, and the illustration is all there is — not a product
photograph, not a box or a board: no bevel, no lip, no rim of light, no
edge, no shadow, no surface behind it, no margin of any colour around
it. Proportions 5:7 portrait. Every position below is measured from
the edges of the image.

Back face layout. This face carries no subject: no figure, no scene, no
picture of anything. The face is matte cardboard printed in deep Elixir
violet (#4B275F), even across the whole face. One thing on it is
lettered, and only one: the words "DOCKERIZED ELIXIR WORKBENCH", set in
beveled chrome capitals with a hard drop shadow, the lettering of the
era, on one line, at foreground size, centred on the width, inside a
rectangle that runs from one tenth of the width to nine tenths of the
width and from one twentieth of the height to one eighth of the height.
Nothing else on the face is lettered: no title, no caption, no
signature, no placeholder text, no glyphs, no words of any kind
anywhere else, neither inside the frames nor outside them.

Two empty inset frames, each outlined by a thin beveled chrome rule,
each filled with one flat near-black tone, unlit, with nothing in it,
each frame three wide by two tall. Both run from twenty hundredths of
the height down to forty hundredths of the height. The left one runs
from six hundredths of the width to forty-eight hundredths of the
width; the right one from fifty-two hundredths of the width to
ninety-four hundredths of the width.

Below them the printed board continues across the middle third of the
face, and one embossed shield is blind-stamped into it, centred on the
width, its centre at three fifths of the height, a quarter of the width
wide, barely darker than the stock it sits on — a watermark rather
than a print, and the one mark on this face. Inside the shield's outline
there is one horizontal bar across its upper third and nothing else.
It carries no lettering.

Across the bottom edge, over the full width, a band of the same board
printed in a darker ink, about a fifteenth of the height of the face.

Palette: Elixir violet (#4B275F) as the dominant colour of the
composition. CRT phosphor haze in the background. Every mark on the
face is printed material or type, and the marks named above are all
of them.
Accent: amber phosphor (#FFB000), the light on the chrome of the
lettering and of the frames' rules.
