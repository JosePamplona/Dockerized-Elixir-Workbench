# ecto — six proposals

Written from the cartridge's NEED.md first, then its README and
DESIGN.md, and nothing else. The era block of the winner goes into
`cover.prompt.txt` verbatim; what follows is the hero paragraph each
era would put in it.

## The need

*Your app has data to keep, and it started without a database.*
Before: an API that computes and forgets. After: a `Repo` on the
adapter you name, migrations, a sandbox for every test, `DATABASE_URL`
in `.env`, the workspace's Postgres after `bake`. Not for changing
your mind later: inserted once.

## The register

**Custodial** — the mechanism holds what you hand it and gives it back
unchanged, for as long as you need. Subtitles vow. Added for this
cartridge: ominous withholds; this keeps.

Title `ECTO`. Subtitle `NOTHING FORGOTTEN`. Badge `4 ADAPTERS` —
Postgres, MySQL, MSSQL, SQLite3, the list you choose from. Accent
**cold jade** or the era's nearest, unused on the shelf. No furniture
unless the era's own column has one.

The counts that are true: **four** adapters; **one** repo; **one**
variable the release reads; **one** sandbox per test; inserted
**once**. The strings a hero may carry at foreground size, one per
hero: `Repo`, `DATABASE_URL`, `migrate`, `insert`. Where something is
handed over, it is data; where it is kept, it is the repo.

## The recommendation

**4, the console entry.** The need has a *handing over* in it —
your data, given to something that will keep it — and the console era
is the one that paints hands and a figure receiving; the shelves
behind say "for as long as you need" without a word. The editorial
ledger is the quiet runner-up.

The shelf as it stands: clustering manual from a home-computer start;
healthcheck2 manual from an editorial start; exdebug console; exdoc
big box; ash big box; coveralls, healthcheck and enhancements starting
over. Console has one cover; html's proposals recommend it too — if
both go there, 3 keeps the custody in one object.
